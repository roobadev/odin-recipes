Noodles

Description

Schezwan noodles are a spicy, Indo-Chinese street food favorite made with stir-fried noodles tossed in a fiery Schezwan sauce, loaded with garlic, chili, and crunchy vegetables. They come together in under 30 minutes and pack a bold, tangy heat in every bite.

Ingredients

  • Noodles
  • Garlic (minced)
  • Soy sauce
  • Schezwan sauce
  • Bell peppers
  • Spring onions

Steps

  1. Boil the noodles according to package instructions, then drain and set aside.
  2. Heat oil in a wok and stir-fry the garlic and bell peppers for 2 minutes.
  3. Add the Schezwan sauce and soy sauce, stirring well to combine.
  4. Toss in the boiled noodles and mix until evenly coated in sauce.
  5. Garnish with spring onions and serve hot.
